# Turbogol

Turbogol is a free, open-source car soccer game that you play in your web browser. You drive a car
that jumps and flies with boost, and you score goals with a big ball in 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3. Without an
account you can play against bots (VS AI), train (free play and shot packs) and play online with
other people in public or private lobbies; with an account you can also play the online CASUAL and
COMPETITIVE matches. Anyone can watch the public replays of the matches played on the community
servers. There are no payments, purchases or ads.

## Sign in with Google

You need an account for CASUAL and COMPETITIVE. You create it with your email and a password, or
with the "Sign in with Google" button (or Discord's).

**Why we ask Google for your data:** only for your Turbogol account: to create it, to sign you in
to it (also when you confirm it is you before an important change) and to send you its emails. For
that we ask Google only for the `openid` and `email` scopes. With them Google gives us your
Google account identifier and your email address, and tells us whether it has confirmed that
address and whether it belongs to a Google Workspace domain. We do not ask for your name, your
picture, your contacts, your Gmail, your Drive or any other Google data.

- **What we keep:** your Google account identifier, to find your account when you sign in with
  Google again, and your email address if Google has confirmed it. Before creating an account with
  Google we ask you whether you already have one: nothing is created until you confirm. We keep them
  until you unlink Google or your account is deleted (if you unlink Google, your account's
  confirmed email stays in it: see below). What is deleted may stay in backup copies for up to
  30 days. If you delete an account that has an active sanction, we keep a keyed hash of them (not
  the data itself) until the sanction ends, 1 year at most, so that an account that uses them later
  gets what was left of the sanction.
- **What your email is for:** it identifies your account, and the game sends your account's emails
  to it: the codes, the links and the security notices. A regular Gmail address becomes your
  account's confirmed email; any other Google address stays a hint until you confirm it with a
  code, and until then we do not write to you there, except for that code. News by email only if
  you ask for it.
- **What we do not do:** we do not sell your data or share it for advertising, we do not keep the
  access tokens Google gives us and we do not send Google your game data. Turbogol's use and
  transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API
  Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
  including the Limited Use requirements.
- **Unlinking Google or deleting your account:** in PROFILE you can unlink Google (if your account
  has another way to sign in) or delete your account (DELETE ACCOUNT, with 14 days to cancel the
  deletion).
  If you unlink Google, we delete your Google account identifier and the email Google gave us as a
  hint; your account's confirmed email stays in it. You can also ask us to delete your account by
  writing to <mailto:privacy@turbo-gol.com>.

The details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://turbo-gol.com/privacy) (sections 2, 4.2, 6 and
12).
