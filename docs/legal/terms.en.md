## 7. Modes and connections

1. **CASUAL.** The connection can be:
   - **Auto** (the preselected one): P2P with other players, or a server with bots if you play
     alone;
   - **P2P**: the same as Auto;
   - **Server**: a server in your region.
2. **P2P means that your device connects directly to the other players' devices,** including people
   you do not know. To set up that connection, each device receives the others' public IP address,
   **even if the match then goes through a Cloudflare relay**. From an IP one can roughly tell which
   city you are in and who your internet provider is. **If you do not want other players to see
   your IP, choose "Server"** as the CASUAL connection, or switch your lobby to "Server".
   COMPETITIVE always uses a server.
3. **COMPETITIVE** (ranked) is always played on a server, with the game's official physics. The
   result changes your rating. The top 100 of each mode, with at least 5 matches in the season,
   appear on a public leaderboard. You can hide your name there **[CODE P25]**.
4. **Custom lobbies.** Whoever creates a lobby is its host: they choose the title, the mode and the
   rules, can set a password and can remove a player. New lobbies are private and P2P: you can
   change both. A public lobby appears in the list with its title and its creator's name. Lobby
   matches never count for ratings.
5. **Experimental features and rules.** They are for accounts, may change or disappear and **count
   for nothing**: no rating, no stats, no records.
6. **Servers.** Server matches are played on Cloudflare servers or on community servers (section
   12). Today the only community server is the project's own, in São Paulo.

## 8. COMPETITIVE: abandons and verification

1. **If you leave a COMPETITIVE match or do not show up, it counts as an abandon.** You cannot
   search for a COMPETITIVE match for:
   - 5 minutes, the first time within 24 hours;
   - 15 minutes, the second;
   - 1 hour, the third;
   - 24 hours, from the fourth on.
2. **If you take 3 seats in P2P rooms without connecting within 10 minutes,** you cannot search for
   P2P matches for 5 minutes. Server matches stay open.
3. The game decides these blocks on its own. You can ask for a person to review them (section 9).
4. **COMPETITIVE matches on community servers are verified** by simulating the match again. If one
   fails verification, or cannot be verified in time, its result may not count.

2. Some casual matches are picked at random to be verified. Only those give points, and they are

3. Your own minutes do not count. Guests and new accounts count for less.
4. Beyond that, the rules of section 11 apply.
