## Message 1

**Project servers in six countries, and the Terms are changing (version 2026-11-05)**

From today, November 5, 2026, Turbogol adds its own servers in other regions:

- **CASUAL and COMPETITIVE matches go to the project server of the region of most of their players,** when it has room: Brazil (South America), Germany (Europe), Malaysia (Asia) and, for now, South Africa (Africa), Australia (Oceania) and the United States (North America). Otherwise they are played on Cloudflare, as before. The ones in service are listed at <https://turbo-gol.com/nodes/status>.
- **Those matches are recorded and published as replays for 30 days,** as already happened in São Paulo. You can hide your name in PROFILE > PRIVACY.
- **A COMPETITIVE match may be verified in another country,** on the server in Brazil, Germany or Malaysia. The verifier receives the replay (your name, your identifier, your team and your controls), not your IP.
- **Your data may be stored in those six countries.** Whoever runs the server you play on sees your IP: today, only the project.
- **The server list** shows only the ones on probation or active, and a host that is not the project shows as "Anfitrión" and a number.
- The team keeps for 30 days, unpublished, a history of the servers' matches, with each player's name.
- In an emergency, if we turn off matches on Cloudflare, each match goes to the server of its region and, if it cannot, to the one in São Paulo.
- In CASUAL you can pick the bots' difficulty of your bots match after 5 minutes.

**Why:** we want less lag for those who play far from South America, and less load on Cloudflare's servers.
