(Terms, section 18.2). The official notice is the Spanish one, [2026-11-13.md](2026-11-13.md);

## Message 1

**Rules for community server hosts, and the Terms are changing (version 2026-11-13)**

From November 13, 2026, the Terms and the Privacy Policy say how the community server network opens to other people. We announce the opening itself separately, in this channel (Terms, section 12.1.2), and it may start with invited people. Until the opening, only the project has servers. From the opening:

- **You can host a server if you are 18 or older:** you ask for it in MY SERVERS, with an account that is 7 days old or more and has a confirmed email, and you declare that you are 18 or older and accept section 12 and Annex B with the button that asks for the code. We do not check the age.
- **Annex B, a new one,** has the clauses for whoever processes the players' data on our behalf, wherever their server is.
- **COMPETITIVE, for now, is played only on project servers or on Cloudflare's.**
- **A CASUAL match may be played on the server of another person,** who sees your name, your account's identifier, your team, your controls and your IP, and your data may reach that server's country. If you do not want that, turn on "Project servers only" in Settings > Gameplay. The match tells you when it is played on one of those servers.
- Some of those CASUAL matches (all of them while the server is on probation) are also verified on a project server, which may be in another country.
- **In the server list,** a host that is not the project shows with their username only if they choose it; otherwise, as "Anfitrión" and a number.
- **Server points** have a cap per day, a bots match with a single player gives no points and the same players repeated are worth less.
- You can report a server when a match on it ends. A single report never sanctions anyone.

**Why:** we want more people to be able to host servers, with clear rules that look after the players.
