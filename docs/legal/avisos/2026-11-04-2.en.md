# Game modes and the Terms are changing (version 2026-11-04-2)

English copy of the notice we posted on Turbogol's Discord server on 4 November 2026 (Terms,
section 18.2). The Discord server is English-only, so this is the text the bot posted there. The
official notice is the Spanish one, [2026-11-04-2.md](2026-11-04-2.md); this copy is informative.
Below, "this channel" is that server's announcements channel.

It is posted in two messages: first the text of "Message 1", then that of "Message 2", copied as
they are from this file (on GitHub, from "Raw"). Each bullet and each paragraph is a single line,
because Discord shows every line break, and each message is under 2,000 characters, which fits in a
Discord message without Nitro. The link goes between `<` and `>` so that Discord does not unfurl it.

## Message 1

**Game modes and the Terms are changing (version 2026-11-04-2)**

From today, November 4, 2026, Turbogol changes how online play works:

- **CASUAL and COMPETITIVE need an account** and are always played on a server, never P2P. Without an account you can play VS AI, train and play in lobbies.
- **CASUAL starts a match as soon as 2 or more people are searching**, and bots fill the free seats. If nobody shows up within 5 minutes, you play against bots on a server.
- **While you wait** you can play against bots with no time limit, or train. When your match is found you join at once, and the local game is lost.
- **We search your region first** and, after 1 minute (2 in COMPETITIVE), every region.
- **More CASUAL matches are played on the São Paulo server:** all those with a majority of players from South America, when there is room, including the ones that used to be P2P. Those matches are recorded and published as public replays for 30 days, as already happened with COMPETITIVE and part of CASUAL. You can hide your name in PROFILE > PRIVACY.
- **Lobbies are always P2P**, public or private: the other players in the lobby see your IP. There are no server lobbies anymore, and a lobby needs 2 people to start: while you wait for someone to join, you can play against bots or train.
- Every day we count, without your account, your name or your IP, how many searches and matches there are and how long people wait.
- If you had the game open from before, reload the page. Lobbies that were open at the moment of the update are closed: after reloading, create your lobby again.

**Why:** with few players online, we want nobody to stare at a waiting screen, and we want matches with people to form as soon as possible.

## Message 2

The Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy have a new version (2026-11-04-2), in force from today. Our Terms (section 18.2) promise notice 30 days before an important change; this time we are publishing it on the day it takes effect, and we apologize. If you do not agree, do not accept it: you can keep playing VS AI and training, and you can delete your account at no cost from PROFILE. Questions: privacy@turbo-gol.com or this channel.
