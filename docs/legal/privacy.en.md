# Turbogol Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-05. In force from November 5, 2026. The Spanish text is the official one; this English translation is informative.

---

## Summary

- **Turbogol is a free game.** There are no payments, purchases or ads. We do not sell your data.
- **To play as a guest we ask you for nothing.** We use your IP address, what happens in the
  match and the browser signals reviewed by the anti-bot check, which runs when you open the
  game.
- **For an account we use your email** (or the identifier of your Discord or Google account), the
  name you choose and, if you use a password, a hash of it. Your password, as you type it, never
  reaches our servers.
- **Some things are public:** your username on the leaderboard (today you cannot hide it), in the
  lobby list if you are the host of a public lobby and, for 30 days, in the replays of matches on
  community servers (COMPETITIVE and CASUAL ones), where you can hide it (section 4.5).
- **In lobbies, which are played P2P, the other players receive your IP address.** CASUAL and
  COMPETITIVE are always played on a server.
- **Whoever operates a community server sees your IP address** and the match. Today those servers
  are only the project's own.
- **We measure visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics,** which, according to Cloudflare, uses no
  cookies. We ask Cloudflare to leave out visitors from the European Union (section 9.4).
- **Your data is stored outside Argentina:** at Cloudflare (including the United States), on the
  project's servers that are in service (in Brazil, Germany and Malaysia and, for now, in South
  Africa, Australia and the United States) and, for the little that reaches the team or our
  mailbox, at Discord and Google (section 5).
- **You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it** by writing to
  <mailto:privacy@turbo-gol.com>. We answer within 10 calendar days at most. You can also delete
  your account from PROFILE (section 12).

### 2.2 What we do not ask for

- Your real name, your date of birth, your phone number, your ID document or payment data.
- Your contacts, your exact location, your camera or your microphone. The page asks the browser
  to block the camera, the microphone and location.
- **There is no chat** between players, whether text, voice or video. The only texts of yours
  that other players can read are your username, your lobby's title and the name of your physics
  presets when a lobby uses them.

### 2.3 Where the data comes from

- **From you:** what you type and what you do in the game.
- **From your browser:** your language and your time zone, when you create the account and when
  you change the language.
- **From Discord or Google,** if you sign in with them: your identifier, your email and whether
  they consider it confirmed. Discord also sends us your basic profile data, which we do not keep.
- **From Cloudflare:** the approximate country and city it derives from your IP address.
- **From community servers:** the match replay.

### 2.4 What happens if you do not give a piece of data, or give a wrong one

- Without an email (or without Discord or Google) there is no account. You can play as a guest,
  unless the anti-bot check rejects you (section 7.4).
- News is optional. Saying no changes nothing in the game.
- Without your IP address there is no internet connection: it is needed to play online.
- If you mistype your email, the codes and notices do not reach you, and we cannot recover your
  account by email.

### 2.5 Old accounts

Some old accounts have only a username and a password, with no email. We keep them all the same
and ask you to add an email or link Discord or Google: the game asks you on screen when you sign
in with your username, and in PROFILE, until you do.

- **The cutoff date.** When it is set, the game shows you the exact date. It is never before
  7 December 2026, and it is always at least 30 days after the day the game starts showing it.
- **From that date,** an account without a confirmed email can no longer sign in with username and
  password. It needs a confirmed email, Discord or Google.
- **The reminder.** If your account already has a confirmed email but still has its old password,
  we send you a reminder 7 days before that date, with a link to create a new password (valid for
  30 minutes). We cannot send it to accounts without an email.
- **If your account already has a confirmed email,** sign in with that email and your password. If
  it does not let you (for example, because your password is the old one and your email came from
  Google), choose a new password with "Forgot your password?".

### 4.3 Other players

- **In each match** they see your name, your car and your team. In a lobby, the other members see
  your name.
- **In a lobby match (P2P),** your device connects directly to the devices of the other players
  in the lobby, even if you do not know them. **Each of them receives your public IP address.**
  With it one can tell, roughly, your city and your internet provider.
  - **When it is P2P:** always in lobbies, public or private, whether you create them or join
    someone else's. CASUAL and COMPETITIVE are never P2P: they are played on a server.
  - **How to avoid it:** do not play in lobbies, or play only with people you know.
  - If the connection with the other players does not succeed within about 10 seconds, the game
    takes you back to the lobby.
  - We tell you once, when you accept the Terms: the notice above the button you accept them with
    ("PLAY AS GUEST", "CREATE ACCOUNT", "NO, CREATE MY ACCOUNT" or "ACCEPT AND PLAY") says that in
    lobbies the other players can see your IP. There is no notice before each lobby match.

### 4.4 Community servers and their hosts

- Some matches are played on community servers ("nodes"). **Today they are only the project's
  own:** in São Paulo (Brazil), Germany and Malaysia, on Hostinger machines, and, for now, in South
  Africa, Australia and the United States, on Google Cloud machines. Each one serves a region:
  South America, Europe, Asia, Africa, Oceania and North America, in that order. The ones in
  service are listed at turbo-gol.com/nodes/status. When it can take them, each one gets the
  CASUAL and COMPETITIVE matches whose players are mostly from its region (on a tie, the region of
  whoever waited longest decides), and also the CASUAL bots matches of whoever searches from its
  region and waited 5 minutes with no rivals. If it cannot, or if that region has no project
  server in service, they are played on Cloudflare servers, never on the project server of another
  region, except in an emergency: if we turn off matches on Cloudflare servers, each match goes to
  the project server of its region and, if that one cannot take it, to the one in São Paulo, and
  waits until one of them has room (Terms 7.6). Lobby matches do not go through them. **Nobody outside
  the project can register a server yet.** Even if the game talks about hosting servers, today you
  cannot register one.
- **Verification, also in another country.** Each COMPETITIVE match on a community server is
  simulated again on a project server that verifies matches: today, those in Brazil, Germany and
  Malaysia. It may be another one than the one that hosted it, in another country: a match played
  in Brazil may be verified in Germany or in Malaysia, and one from South Africa, Australia or the
  United States on any of the three. That server receives the replay, with each player's name,
  player identifier, team and controls, but not their IP. CASUAL matches on a project server are
  not verified, and the servers in South Africa, Australia and the United States never verify
  matches.
- **When the network opens to other people** (we will announce it), whoever operates a server will
  see your name, your player identifier (the same in all your matches), your controls, what happens
  in the match and your IP address. The conditions for hosts (section 12 of the Terms) forbid them
  to keep or use that data for anything else.
- **About hosts** we keep their username, each server's data (name, region, public address, which
  may be an IP, capacity, version, machine data and how many matches it verified, lost or failed),
  the IP addresses their servers connect from (30 days), a history of their servers and of their
  matches (30 days, section 6) and their points. A server stays in our records even if it is shut
  down or we retire it. Today that applies only to the project's account.
- **It is public,** for anyone who asks for it at turbo-gol.com/nodes/status: each server in
  service (on probation or active), with who runs it, and the points of the last 30 days of the 50
  hosts who earned the most (section 4.5). A project account is shown with its username; any other
  host as "Anfitrión" (Spanish for "host") and a number, never with their username. If the host
  does not choose a name for their server, the server is called by its region and a number.

## 10. Security

- **Your password, as you type it, does not reach our servers.** Your browser turns it into a key.
  We keep only a hash of that key, with a salt. On accounts that were created, changed their
  password or signed in with it since 30 October 2026,
  the hash also carries a secret that is not in the database.
- **The game uses HTTPS.** The site asks your browser to always use HTTPS (HSTS). The fonts, the
  images and the styles come from turbo-gol.com, and the only scripts from other sites are
  Cloudflare's (Turnstile and Web Analytics).
- **Session cookies** travel encrypted over HTTPS and the page's code cannot read them.
- **The abuse counters that persist** keep hashes with a secret key, which nobody can reverse
  without that key; they do not keep your email, your name or your IP. The very short ones use your
  IP or your session or account identifier, and delete themselves (section 6).
- **We tell you by email** every time your password or your email changes, or when you link or
  unlink Discord or Google. An email change can be undone for 7 days.
- **Our emails have no images,** tracking pixels or tracked links.
- **Technical logs never keep your password** or the body of what you send to the server (what you
  type in forms). Our own log lines carry no IP, email or password. Only a few carry the username of a
  team account, or the identifier of an account that was deleted (in full if it was a host; the
  first 6 characters in other cases).
- **Technical logs keep the addresses requested.** Some carry an identifier: the single-use code
  from Discord or Google when you come back to Turbogol, a lobby's code, a match's and, when we
  start sending news, the identifier of the unsubscribe link, which does not change until you
  unsubscribe through that link. The links in your account's emails carry their code after the
  "#", which your browser does not send to the server, and so it does not end up in the logs.
- **Team sign-ins** (the accounts that maintain the project) generate an alert in an internal
  channel and in our logs, with the username, the account identifier, the method and the country,
  never the IP or the email.

No system is perfect. **If something happens that may affect you, we tell you** and we report it to
the authority when the law requires it. If you find a security flaw, write to us at
<mailto:privacy@turbo-gol.com>. If you have a GitHub account, you can also use the repository's private
security advisory (<https://github.com/DonJaimeHash/turbo-gol/security>).

### 12.1 How to ask for it

- **We give you a receipt.** If you ask from PROFILE, you see it on the screen at that moment

### 12.2 What happens

This is what happens if you ask from PROFILE. If you ask by email, there is no 14-day wait
(section 12.1 and point 4 below).

1. **At that moment:**
   - you are signed out on every device;
   - you leave the leaderboard (the list refreshes every 30 seconds);
   - your news subscription is cancelled;
   - if your account has a confirmed email, we send you an email with a link to cancel the
     deletion.
2. **You have 14 days to cancel the deletion.** Sign in to your account and choose KEEP MY ACCOUNT,
   or use the link in the email. During those 14 days your name stays in public replays, unless you
   already hid it in PROFILE > PRIVACY before asking for the deletion (during those 14 days you
   cannot sign in to change it without cancelling the deletion).
3. **After 14 days we delete the account** (the automatic deletion runs once a day, so it may
   happen up to one day later) and everything it holds: your email, your name, your password, your
   links to Discord and Google, your devices, your rating, your stats, your options, your available
   points and host items and your record of news and acceptances (a minimal proof of the latter
   remains: see below).
4. **If you do not want to wait the 14 days,** write to us at <mailto:privacy@turbo-gol.com> and we delete it within 5
   business days at most.

### 12.3 What remains afterwards

- **The account's identifier** (a random number), with no name or email, so that it is never given

- **A minimal proof of what you accepted and withdrew.** For each text you accepted and each news

  without where it came from. It is kept for 2 years from the deletion and serves to prove what you
  asked for.
- **Copies that expire on their own:**
  - the replays of your matches: the public sees them with your name hidden, but our internal copy
    keeps your name and your player identifier until it expires; the seats you took in them and the
    stars you gave, with your account's identifier and never your name; and the host point records
    of those matches, also with the identifier and without your name: up to
    30 days;
  - the matches of the servers' internal history: your name and your identifier leave it when the
    account is deleted, and the match stays, without them, for up to 30 days;
  - the team's alert lines that carry your account's identifier (up to 30 days in the internal
    history and 90 days on Discord, section 6);
  - Cloudflare's technical logs (7 days) and those of the machines of the project's servers
    (section 6);
  - the email sending log (up to 30 days);
  - Cloudflare's backups (up to 30 days).
- **If you were a host:** today only the project has servers, so this does not yet affect any
  player. When it opens to other people: the record of how many points your servers earned (which

- **What you wrote to us** and our answers, for the period in section 6.
- **Sanction marks** (below).

### 12.4 Sanction marks

If you delete an account that has an active sanction (a suspension, a queue block, matches
abandoned in the last 24 hours that still count, or an unfinished COMPETITIVE match):

- we keep a **hash with a secret key** (HMAC), which nobody can reverse without that key, of its
  email, of the previous email if you changed it less than 7 days before, of the email that Discord
  or Google gave us as a hint and of its Discord and Google identifiers;
- **we do not keep the email or the identifiers** themselves;
- the hash lasts **until the sanction ends, and 1 year at most**. A suspension with no end date
  counts as 1 year;
- if that email, that Discord account or that Google account is later used on a Turbogol account
  (when it is created, when its email is added or changed, or when Discord or Google is linked),
  **that account receives what was left of the sanction:** it cannot play COMPETITIVE until the
  date the suspension, the block or the unfinished match would have ended, and the abandoned matches that
  still counted count again. The rest of the game stays open;
- today we do not tell you at that moment: you see the COMPETITIVE block in the game, but not that
  it comes from a mark.

It is so that nobody deletes their account and comes back with a clean record. It is pseudonymized
data: with the key, we can compare it.

### 12.5 If you unlink Discord or Google

We delete your identifier for that service and the email that service gave us as a hint. Your
account's confirmed email stays in it, even if we took it from Google. An email hint is also
deleted when you confirm it with a code, when another account confirms that address or when you
delete your account.
